# Theme Card: `samurai` — Samurai Fortress & Bamboo Garden

## Vibe & Atmosphere
Feudal mountain stronghold: sweeping `deepslate_bricks` castle roofs with crimson upturned eaves, weathered `calcite` plaster over dark-oak timber framing, raked zen courtyards, dense bamboo groves, and misty mountain waterfalls.

## Palette & Gradient Roles (`mckit.get_theme('samurai')`)
- **Primary Wood:** `dark_oak` (`dark_oak_wood`, `dark_oak_planks`, `dark_oak_stairs`, `dark_oak_slab`, `dark_oak_fence`)
- **Accent Timber:** `spruce` (`spruce_planks`, `stripped_spruce_log`, `spruce_trapdoor`)
- **Lacquer / Banner Accent:** `crimson` (`crimson_hyphae`, `crimson_planks`, `crimson_slab`) + `red_wool` / `black_wool`
- **Roofs & Plinths:** `deepslate_bricks` + `polished_deepslate`; recessed `calcite` / `diorite` walls
- **Cliff & Path Mix:** `deepslate` → `cobbled_deepslate` → `tuff` → `andesite` → `stone`; `mossy_stone_bricks`
- **Trees & Flora:** `japanese_pine` (bonsai cloud pads), `bamboo_clump`, `autumn_maple`, `sakura` accent; `fern`, `large_fern`, `red_tulip`, `moss_carpet`
- **Lighting:** `lantern`, `ochre_froglight`, `campfire`

## Signature Landmarks & Features
1. **3-Tier Tenshu Keep / Dojo Pagoda** on a stepped stone-brick fortress plinth (`props.place_pagoda`).
2. **Crimson Torii Gate** (`props.place_torii_gate`) + **Arched Crimson Bridge** (`props.place_bridge`) over a mountain stream/pond.
3. **Zen Raked Courtyard** (`props.place_zen_garden`), **Samurai Encampment** (`props.place_campfire_camp` with red/black tents), and **Windswept Bonsai Pines**.
