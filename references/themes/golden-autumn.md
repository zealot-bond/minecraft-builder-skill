# Theme Card: `golden_autumn` — Golden Autumn Valley

## Vibe & Atmosphere
Sunlit harvest valley bathed in golden-amber foliage (`birch_leaves`, `oak_leaves`, `flowering_azalea_leaves`), warm spruce & acacia timber roofs, mossy cobblestone arch bridges, crackling campfires, and berry-lined woodland trails.

## Palette & Gradient Roles (`mckit.get_theme('golden_autumn')`)
- **Primary Wood:** `spruce` (`spruce_wood`, `spruce_planks`, `spruce_stairs`, `spruce_slab`, `spruce_trapdoor`, `spruce_fence`)
- **Accent Wood & Warm Roofs:** `oak` + `acacia` (`acacia_stairs`, `acacia_slab`, `stripped_birch_wood`)
- **Stone & Cliff Gradient:** `deepslate` → `tuff` → `granite` → `andesite` → `stone`; `cobblestone` & `mossy_cobblestone`
- **Ground & Paths:** `grass_block`, `podzol`, `coarse_dirt`, `yellow_terracotta` leaf litter; `dirt_path`, `gravel`, `cobblestone`
- **Trees & Flora:** `autumn_maple`, `birch_grove`, `giant_oak`, `spruce`; `sunflower`, `orange_tulip`, `dandelion`, `sweet_berry_bush`, `fern`
- **Lighting:** `lantern`, `ochre_froglight`, `campfire`, `yellow_stained_glass_pane`

## Signature Landmarks & Features
1. **Harvest Watchtower / Woodland Shrine** (`props.place_pagoda` or custom mill/watchtower) on a granite-and-moss crag.
2. **Timber Arch Bridge** (`props.place_bridge`) & **Fishing Pier** (`props.place_dock`) over a reed-fringed mirror pond.
3. **Golden Maple & Giant Oak Swing Trees** (`trees.place_tree(..., with_swing_branch=True)`), **Harvest Market Stalls**, and **Travelers' Campfire Clearing**.
