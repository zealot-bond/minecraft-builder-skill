# Theme Card: `vanilla_plus` — Overgrown Meadow Haven

## Vibe & Atmosphere
Classic survival Minecraft elevated to showcase craft: towering ancient oaks with wooden chain swings, mossy stone-brick arches, bubbling spring waterfalls, cozy striped market stalls, and warm lantern-lit campsites.

## Palette & Gradient Roles (`mckit.get_theme('vanilla_plus')`)
- **Primary Wood:** `oak` (`oak_wood`, `oak_planks`, `oak_stairs`, `oak_slab`, `oak_fence`, `oak_trapdoor`)
- **Accent Timber & Roofs:** `spruce` (`spruce_wood`, `spruce_planks`, `spruce_stairs`, `spruce_slab`, `stripped_spruce_log`) + `stripped_birch_wood` plaster
- **Stone & Cliff Gradient:** `deepslate` → `tuff` → `cobblestone` → `andesite` → `stone` → `mossy_cobblestone`; `stone_bricks` & `mossy_stone_bricks`
- **Paths & Shore:** `dirt_path`, `coarse_dirt`, `gravel`, `cobblestone`; `sand`, `clay`, `mud`, `moss_block`
- **Trees & Flora:** `giant_oak`, `weeping_willow`, `birch_grove`, `spruce`; `poppy`, `cornflower`, `oxeye_daisy`, `dandelion`, `fern`, `short_grass`
- **Lighting:** `lantern`, `verdant_froglight`, `campfire`

## Signature Landmarks & Features
1. **3-Tier Timber & Stone Watchtower / Guild Shrine** (`props.place_pagoda`) on a mossy crag with waterfall.
2. **Spruce & Stone-Brick Arch Bridge** (`props.place_bridge`), **Giant Ancient Oak Swing Trees**, **Village Market Stalls**, **Fishing Pier**, and **Adventurer Campfire Clearing**.
