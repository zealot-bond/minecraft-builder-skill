# Theme Card: `fantasy` — Enchanted Amethyst Grove

## Vibe & Atmosphere
Mystical twilight glade where twisted dark-oak & warped-wood trees shelter glowing amethyst crystal outcrops, dark-prismarine shrines, luminous spring ponds, and soul-lantern bridges.

## Palette & Gradient Roles (`mckit.get_theme('fantasy')`)
- **Primary Wood:** `dark_oak` + `crimson` / `warped` (`warped_planks`, `warped_stairs`, `warped_slab`, `crimson_hyphae`)
- **Roofs & Plinths:** `dark_prismarine` + `deepslate_bricks`; `calcite` & `amethyst_block` walls/crystals
- **Cliff Gradient:** `obsidian` → `crying_obsidian` → `deepslate` → `tuff` → `amethyst_block` → `calcite`
- **Trees & Flora:** `sakura`, `weeping_willow`, `azalea`, `neon_tree`; `flowering_azalea_leaves`, `allium`, `blue_orchid`, `pink_petals`, `moss_carpet`
- **Lighting:** `soul_lantern`, `pearlescent_froglight`, `purple_stained_glass`, `soul_campfire`

## Signature Landmarks & Features
1. **Prismarine & Amethyst Spire / Elven Sanctuary** (`props.place_pagoda` or `place_holo_spire`).
2. **Warped-Wood Arch Bridge** over a glowing spring pond, **Amethyst Crystal Boulders**, **Enchanted Swing Trees**, and **Twilight Camp**.
