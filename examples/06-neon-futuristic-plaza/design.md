# Aether Core — Neon Solarpunk Plaza (`futuristic`)

## 1. Brief
- **Dimensions:** `32 x 34 x 32` standalone sci-fi hub island (`futuristic` theme).
- **Spawn Point:** `(16, 10, 27)` looking North toward `(16, 16, 14)` directly at the **Holo Energy Spire** framed by glowing neon pylons and bioluminescent gardens.
- **Purpose:** Compact PvP / sci-fi lobby hub proving the `mckit` toolkit & theme system shifts effortlessly from organic Japanese sanctuaries to sleek futuristic architecture.

## 2. Inspiration Digest
1. **Solarpunk & Sci-Fi Server Hubs (GenMC / BuiltByBit):** High-contrast pairing of bright `smooth_quartz` ribs against dark `deepslate_tiles` structural frames, lit by `sea_lantern` conduits and `cyan_stained_glass` energy rings.
2. **Bioluminescent Xenobotany:** Pairing sleek architecture with lush alien flora (`warped_hyphae` trunks, `flowering_azalea_leaves`, `blue_orchid`, `soul_lantern` drops) so the plaza feels alive rather than sterile concrete.

## 3. Palette (`futuristic` theme card)
- **Primary Structural:** `smooth_quartz`, `quartz_bricks`, `deepslate_tiles`, `polished_deepslate`
- **Energy & Glass:** `sea_lantern`, `cyan_stained_glass`, `cyan_stained_glass_pane`, `soul_lantern`, `end_rod`
- **Bioluminescent Flora:** `warped_hyphae`, `azalea_leaves`, `flowering_azalea_leaves`, `moss_block`, `blue_orchid`, `allium`
